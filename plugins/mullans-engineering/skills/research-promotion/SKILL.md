# Research promotion

Codify reusable facts and contracts from this session's experimental work. A
milestone qualifies when supported knowledge can inform subsequent work, even
while other investigation questions remain open. Use the project's existing
evidence, reference and authorization conventions.

## 1. Select the session's candidates

Identify the authorized workstream, experiment records written or updated this
session, and candidate claims. Reconcile the experiment records first so evidence,
reasoning and limits survive independently of promotion. Assume older experiments
have already been reviewed; inspect them only as decisive supporting sources or
to resolve a specific ambiguity. Review a broader corpus only when requested.

Each candidate must answer a reusable reference question with a precise supported
fact or contract. Static facts, scoped live observations, attributed source claims,
derived relationships and bounded negative results can qualify. Keep hypotheses,
unresolved interpretations, chronology, trial procedures and temporary state in
experiments or their designated procedural/continuation owners. A failed read or
inadequate coverage does not establish absence.

Done: every candidate has a precise claim, decisive evidence, applicability and
material limits, or an explicit reason to remain in the experiment. If none
qualify, report that outcome without creating reference content.

## 2. Reconcile with maintained knowledge

Read relevant owning references and their cited support. Check that evidence
supports the exact claim, rather than a broader behavior, cause or capability.
Copied summaries are not independent corroboration. A mapped binding does not
establish safe invocation; a tested value does not establish a maximum.

Classify each candidate as an addition, refinement, clear supersession, duplicate
or conflict. Clear supersession is routine only when the evidence unambiguously
answers the same question within the same scope. Preserve the original observation
in its experiment and make its replacement discoverable.

For a contradiction or ambiguous resolution, hold the candidate and dependent
claims. Flag the affected existing reference with a precise source-linked
revalidation warning without replacing its conclusion. Finish reviewing the batch,
then present all conflicts together: existing claim, new evidence and scope,
recommended resolution with reasoning, potential outcomes and decision needed.
Wait for the user's resolution before publishing contested conclusions.
Independent, uncontested candidates may proceed while those decisions are pending.

Done: every candidate has a disposition and owner; all conflicts and dependent
claims are identified for one collective user briefing.

## 3. Draft and review reference units

Use existing reference formats and stable IDs. If conventions are absent, create
only a minimal subject page and discovery route for the qualifying material.
Write the supported fact or contract with precise evidence links, applicability,
confidence where locally required, and factual limits. Keep historical receipts
and reasoning in the experiment; include provenance only as needed to interpret
the fact. Reusable operating procedures belong in procedural documentation.

Perform a distinct semantic review pass against the decisive evidence and existing
references. Check every factual sentence or wholly supported table group. Check
numbers, identities, conditions, derived reasoning and supersession scope; source
hashes and link checks cannot establish an accurate paraphrase.

The session agent may review routine promotion. Require an independent reviewer
for sufficiently complex cases, such as interdependent claims, difficult evidence
interpretation or consequential contract changes that exceed a routine review.
If that review is unavailable, retain the draft in the experiment and report the
missing review. Reviewer agreement does not replace a user decision on conflicts.

Done: each publishable unit has passed semantic review, with any required
independent review complete; unsupported or contested material remains held.

## 4. Publish and report

Apply reviewed routine promotion within existing authorization. Preserve unrelated
work, stable finding identities, evidence paths and meaningful limits. Repair
relevant navigation and run established safe citation, link and catalog checks.
Inspect the diff for historical logging, procedures, temporary state or unsupported
claims entering references. Commit according to project instructions.

Record the promotion disposition, reference links, review and checks in the owning
experiment, not as a reference-page activity log. Report promoted findings, held
items and material gaps; present all pending conflicts in one briefing. An
investigation closeout or handoff may proceed with unpromoted findings recorded.

For splitting, consolidation or reference-organization audits, use
`maintain-research-docs` and the project's maintenance approval process. Routine
promotion does not reset organizational review baselines. Policy changes and
substantial restructuring require a user decision unless already authorized.

Done: published units and navigation are verified, experiment dispositions are
recorded, and unresolved decisions or checks are reported explicitly.
